Updated: My Minecraft Territory

A Minecraft territory update with a TNT shower, wildfire, expanded rollercoaster, jungle-tree hideout, and underground base.

You may have seen my old Minecraft territory, but I’ve updated it, and the rollercoaster changed in a way that doesn’t match the plans I had before. I’m assuming you read the old post, but if you haven’t, go read it here! Now, it’s time to look at what happened.

Events

TNT shower

My cousin wanted to see a giant TNT explosion, so I used a command to make a 30 × 30 × 30 cube of TNT and ignited it with a polished blackstone button. It created a rain of ignited and passive TNT that littered an area at least 1,000 × 1,000 blocks—a real landmark of the disaster, and a lot of fun! The rollercoaster, jungle trees, and cherry tree biome were damaged.

Minecraft wildfire

While placing lava to destroy the minecarts, I set fire to a cherry tree. The fire destroyed about 10% of my cherry tree biome, but I replanted the burnt trees.

Things

Rollercoaster

The rollercoaster includes everything from the old post. In the cherry tree biome, I built a treetop path leading up a slope and along a long route to a big, smiling grapefruit, where I made a little house. A path returns to the main territory, followed by a jump to another treetop path and a bridge climbing the big jungle trees.

After falling back down, there is a loop leading to the second treetop path and the slope toward the grapefruit. This time, the minecarts go down the slope to the first treetop bridge. Finally, a small slope leads to the tunnel from the first post. The minecarts keep going in and out of the tunnel—an infinite loop!

Jungle trees

The jungle trees were also made for a base. I placed rollercoaster minecarts among them, and they became a hideout. The only way up was by vines, and only some trees could be climbed. The TNT rain caused one tree to float above the ground, connected to the other trees and the ground only by vines, with no trunk touching the ground.

Underground base

I carved this base out of rock using TNT because I hadn’t learned the fill command when I made it. It has a bedroom, farm, and ranch—enough to hide in during a hostile-mob apocalypse—and is made from very durable netherite blocks. It is about 50 blocks underground, and I built a five-level beacon there.

It is still not ready to live in. It is missing a roof, walls, survival items, and luxury items.

Summary

My new territory is better than ever! I may add another update soon, so keep watch!
