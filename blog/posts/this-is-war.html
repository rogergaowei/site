This is WARRRRRR!!!!!!!

LEGO City's walls, cannons, battleships, plane, and drone, and what each did in the war.

Published by Roger.

Recently, World War I broke out in the LEGO world, and it caused LEGO City to have to use some of its most advanced defense systems, although it still managed to win.

The following table says the type of war mechanic and what it did:

System nameSystem type and how it does itWhy it was createdSpecialWhat it did in the war:Did it survive?Defense positionMilitary group
Walls Defense; blocks enemy troops from getting into the city LEGO City had been overrun before, and the mayor didn’t want that to happen again… Toughest; built out of diamond (strongest mineral) and corundum (second strongest mineral) Block off 10 of the 11 raids (with the help of the cannons) Despite getting overrun by enemies (they didn’t beat the LEGO City military), the wall’s toughness allowed %99 to survive. Outer Ground defense
Cannons Defense and offense; stops enemies from approaching the walls. On the offensive side, the cannons can destroy military bases. The mayor realized that although the cannons could be destroyed when the enemy overrun the walls, the cannons would do enough to the enemy so that it would be worth it, even if they were destroyed. The plane Offense and defense; the plane aggressively attacks enemy troops that are approaching to defend the city. It participates in dogfights with the other planes and wins. After it has taken down the other enemy planes, the plane attacks the enemy. Other countries had airplanes, too. Even though the other airplanes were flimsy, the anti-aircraft systems needed more science to be created. The only way to stop these planes from damaging LEGO City was to make their own airplane. Has space for many men (specifically paratroopers) to make a surprise attack at night. The plane’s stealthiness (very camouflaged, no radar detection, no sound) helps with the surprise attacks. Defeat the other planes and wreck havoc on the enemy. Survived, except the other planes damaged it, so it almost lost the battle with the last plane. Everywhere (the plane can fly, okay?) Aerial
Drone Offense, launches missiles at enemy assets. It can also launch bombs and torpedoes to recreate the bombing of Pearl Harbor. It became clear that the plane needed help to recreate the bombing on Pearl Harbor on a country with 1,000 battleships stationed in a singular port… Can choose what it uses for attacks (machine guns, bombs, conventional missiles, unconventional missiles, torpedos) Defeat literally ONE THOUSAND battleships when it recreated Pearl Harbor getting bombed (Actually, in the LEGO world, it was called The Bombing of Terror Harbor (hey, the harbor had 1,000 battleships!)) Except for getting heavily damaged in the bombing of Terror Harbor, it stayed intact. (The wings weren’t damaged, so the drone glided for 7 days until it got recaptured by LEGO City. The drone was damaged in the tail, communications system, fuel tank, and engine.) Aerial

Here are the main milestones of the war:

  • The bombing of Terror Harbor: This move stopped the Terror Harbor fleet from destroying the Lego City fleet.
  • The First Nuke: LEGO City invented the nuke to drop on the final Axis Power* to make it surrender, so the war would end. (This action is much like when USA dropped the first nuke on Japan to make it surrender, ending the actual WWII.)
