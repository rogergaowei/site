Azam's Birthday Party at Sloomoo

A birthday party at Sloomoo with slime, instant snow, DIY slime, slime pools, and a party room.

September 5, 2026

Today, I went to Azam’s birthday party at Sloomoo. First, we went on a tour of the kitchen, where the slime was made. It also looked like a warehouse because it made slime for three locations. We also saw how instant snow was made: adding water to a substance resulted in the “snow,” basically regular powdered snow that isn’t cold.

Then, we entered the “Sloomooverse,” a universe of different kinds of slime. We first went to an area where all visitors put a piece of slime on a wall. In some parts, the slime extended up to 5 to 7 feet just because of the slime! Also, watching the slime flow down was satisfying.

Then, we saw and played with a lot of different slimes. The one I liked the most was the Icee-type slime, which, when spread out, made a web like the ones orb spiders weave. I think one of the ingredients in the Icee-type slime was instant snow.

There was also a machine to tell you which Sloomoo™ mascot you were most like, but I didn’t get a chance to see which one I was. Then, we went to a room with a glass wall where the person hit by the slime got to aim and fire it again. One piece of slime, if there hadn’t been a glass wall, would have hit my heart! (POV: I died.)

Then, we went to see even more types of slime. My favorite was still the Icee-type slime, which, when spread out, made a ginormous spiderweb. There was also another slime that could make a big bubble. In the mold area, we could put Icee slime into molds to create shapes.

We went into a special area where we first touched the slime to create music, then played with sand to make mountains. I saw a slime shower area where slime was poured onto other people. I was reluctant to go to the slime shower because I thought the slime would fall directly onto us and cover my clothes, which I didn’t want to happen.

Finally, we went to our own area, where we made DIY slime. My first one was an Icee-type slime with a lychee scent and gold and a type of teal for the colors.

After the tour was finished, we went to an area where we walked on slime. I made an interconnecting trail from one side of the slime pool to the other. Unfortunately, many people stepped on it, so I had to redo it over and over again. I would just finish paving the trail, and then I’d have to redo it all over again.

After this was finished, I put my shoes and socks back on, but then I couldn’t find the first slime. I got one of the employees to redo it, and this time it had two types of teal and blue. It was still an Icee-type slime, and its scent was Watermelon Patch. I liked this one more.

Finally, we went to the party room, where I ate some fruit and drank some Sprite. I didn’t get any pizza because there were no cheese or pepperoni pizzas.

Finally, the birthday party was over. This birthday party was a really good one!
