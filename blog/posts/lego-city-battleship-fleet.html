The LEGO City Battleship Fleet

Four battleships, each with a different role in LEGO City's defense and offense system.

October 9, 2026 · Published by Roger.

As countries around LEGO City grew, LEGO City decided that it needed a good defense and offense system. Battleships are one part of that system.

There are four battleships, each with a different role. Here is a grid of them:

Heavy assault1Helped win the battle of IranHeavy attack; nobody beats this ship in a one-on-one battleMain gun = lifeboat; torpedo launchers; most cannons; autopilot system; air force carrier; most abilities.Government funds made it.

Though the battleships already seem really strong, they function really well, too. While patrolling the seas, the light assault battleship managed to take down a sneak attack mission from the enemy. The two beginning ships also somehow managed to defeat Iran's ships without sinking.
