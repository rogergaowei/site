Digging Ginseng (人参)

Digging up three ginseng plants with Dad, his high school classmates, their daughter, and a guide.

July 14, 2025 · Published by Roger.

Today, my dad and I went to dig ginseng with two of my dad’s high school classmates and their daughter. Ginseng is a plant that grows in cool, mountainous, forested regions of the Korean Peninsula, northeastern China (Manchuria), and the Russian Far East.

To reach the digging spot, we went down a dirt path about 200 meters (660 feet) long. When we arrived, we started digging with the help of a guide.

First, we dug into the soil surrounding each plant to find its rhizome. After locating the rhizome, we loosened the soil. Finally, we pulled the plant out.
